Week 2: Overview of Business Intelligence & Decision Support (Part 2) 第 2 周：商业智能与决策支持概述（二）

Step-by-Step Guide 分步指南

1. The DSS Concept

A Decision Support System (DSS) is an interactive, flexible computer-based information system designed to help managers solve semi-structured or unstructured decision problems by utilizing data, mathematical models, and an intuitive user interface.

2. Business Intelligence Framework

A Business Intelligence (BI) framework has four major components: the **Data Warehouse** (central repository), **Business Analytics** (queries, reporting, data mining), **Business Performance Management (BPM)** (portfolio scoring), and the **User Interface** (interactive dashboards).

1. 决策支持系统 (DSS) 概念

决策支持系统 (DSS) 是一种交互式、灵活的、基于计算机的信息系统，旨在通过利用数据、数学模型和直观的用户界面，帮助管理人员解决半结构化或非结构化的决策问题。

2. 商业智能 (BI) 框架

商业智能 (BI) 框架有四个主要组成部分：**数据仓库**（核心库）、**商业分析**（查询、报表、数据挖掘）、**业务绩效管理 (BPM)**（组合评分）和**用户界面**（交互式仪表板）。

Tips 提示

While DSS targets specific decision tasks for individual managers, BI has a broader, enterprise-wide focus prioritizing query, reporting, and historical trend analysis.
DSS 针对单个管理人员的特定决策任务，而 BI 则具有更广泛、企业级的关注度，优先考虑查询、报表和历史趋势分析。
