Week 3: Foundation & Technologies for Decision Making (Part 1) 第 3 周：决策制定基础与技术（一）

Step-by-Step Guide 分步指南

1. Herbert Simon's Decision Process

Herbert Simon defined four fundamental phases of decision-making: Intelligence (scanning env to find problems), Design (developing alternative solutions), Choice (selecting an alternative), and Implementation (putting solution into action).

2. DSS Support Mechanisms

Decision Support Systems provide tailored support for each phase: Intelligence (data queries, OLAP, data mining), Design (mathematical modeling, forecasting), Choice (decision matrices, multi-criteria analysis), and Implementation (reporting).

1. 别特·西蒙决策过程

赫伯特·西蒙定义了决策制定的四个基本阶段：情报阶段（扫描环境发现问题）、设计阶段（开发备选方案）、抉择阶段（选择备选方案）和实施阶段（将方案付诸行动）。

2. DSS 支持机制

决策支持系统针对每个阶段提供定制支持：情报阶段（数据查询、OLAP、数据挖掘）、设计阶段（数学建模、预测）、抉择阶段（决策矩阵、多准则分析）和实施阶段（报表报告）。
