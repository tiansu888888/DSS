Week 1: Overview of Business Intelligence & Decision Support (Part 1) 第 1 周：商业智能与决策支持概述（一）

Step-by-Step Guide 分步指南

1. Changing Business Environment

The modern business environment is highly competitive, volatile, and complex. Organizations face constant changes in markets, consumer demands, and technologies, creating a critical need for rapid, computerized decision support systems to maintain a competitive edge.

2. Managerial Decision Making

Managerial decisions are divided into three levels: Strategic (long-term planning), Tactical (mid-level resource control), and Operational (daily task execution). Semi-structured and unstructured decisions require advanced information system support.

1. 快速变化的商业环境

现代商业环境极具竞争性、波动性和复杂性。企业面临着市场、消费者需求和技术的不断变化，这迫切需要计算机化的决策支持系统来做出快速反应，以保持核心竞争力。

2. 管理决策制定

管理决策分为三个层级：战略级（长期规划）、战术级（中层资源控制）和操作/运行级（日常任务执行）。半结构化和非结构化决策需要高级信息系统的支持。

Tips 提示

Always remember the chronological levels of decision-making: Strategic -> Tactical -> Operational. Strategic is handled by senior executive managers, operational by supervisors.
始终记住决策制定的层级顺序：战略级 -> 战术级 -> 操作级。战略级由高管处理，操作级由基层主管处理。
