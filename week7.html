Step-by-Step Guide 分步指南

1. Optimization Models

Models represent a simplified reality. Optimization models help decision makers find the best possible solution. Linear programming is a common optimization technique used to maximize profits or minimize costs under resource limitations.

2. Decision Variables and Constraints

Linear programming formulations require identifying: the **Objective Function** (the mathematical expression to maximize/minimize), **Decision Variables** (unknown values to solve), and **Constraints** (inequality limits representing boundaries).

1. 优化模型

模型代表简化的现实。优化模型帮助决策者找到最佳解决方案。线性规划是常用的优化技术，用于在资源限制下实现利润最大化或成本最小化。

2. 决策变量与约束条件

线性规划公式构建需要确定：**目标函数**（实现最大化/最小化的数学表达式）、**决策变量**（待求解的未知值）和**约束条件**（代表物理限制的不等式边界）。
