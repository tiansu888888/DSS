Week 5: Knowledge Management 第 5 周：知识管理

Step-by-Step Guide 分步指南

1. Tacit vs. Explicit Knowledge

Knowledge in organizations is divided into two types: **Explicit knowledge** (structured, written down in manuals, easily transferable) and **Tacit knowledge** (unstructured, experience-based, difficult to formalize or communicate).

2. Information Technology in KM

IT supports Knowledge Management (KM) through document stores, intranet search, expert directories, and modern cognitive tools like AI chat engines (ChatGPT, Poe, and DeepSeek) which aid in capturing and summarizing tacit knowledge structures.

1. 隐性知识与显性知识

组织中的知识分为两类：**显性知识**（结构化的、记录在手册中的、易于传递的）和**隐性知识**（非结构化的、基于个人经验的、难以形式化或沟通传授的）。

2. 知识管理中的信息技术

IT 通过文档存储、内部网搜索、专家目录以及现代认知工具（如 AI 聊天引擎：ChatGPT、Poe 和 DeepSeek）来支持知识管理 (KM)，这些工具有助于捕获和总结隐性知识结构。

Tips 提示

Understand the difference: Explicit is codified (like a class textbook), Tacit is personal (like a master craftsman's instinct). AI tools help turn tacit knowledge into explicit text.
理解其区别：显性是法定的/公式化的（如教科书），隐性是个人经验的（如大师级工匠的直觉）。AI 工具有助于将隐性知识转化为显性文本。
