Week 10: Designing Decision Support System (Part 1) 第 10 周：设计决策支持系统（一）

Step-by-Step Guide 分步指南

1. Planning for DSS

Planning for a Decision Support System involves scoping user requirements, conducting a feasibility study (economic, organizational, technical), and defining clear system boundaries to ensure alignment with business goals.

2. User Requirements and Scopes

Requirements gathering utilizes interviews and document analysis to model decision parameters. System scope definition outlines what the DSS will deliver, preventing scope creep during development phases.

1. DSS 规划

规划决策支持系统涉及确定用户需求范围、进行可行性研究（经济、组织、技术）以及界定清晰的系统边界，以确保与业务目标保持一致。

2. 用户需求与系统范围

需求收集利用访谈和文档分析来模拟决策参数。系统范围定义概述了 DSS 将交付的内容，从而防止在开发阶段发生范围蔓延。

Tips 提示

Never skip feasibility analysis in planning. Economic feasibility determines ROI, technical feasibility verifies if tools (like Power BI) can perform the required logic.
在规划中切勿跳过可行性分析。经济可行性决定了投资回报率 (ROI)，技术可行性则验证了工具（如 Power BI）是否能够执行所需的逻辑。
